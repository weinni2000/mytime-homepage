Adds an interactive **radial orbital timeline** to the website at `/orbital_frontend_view`.

Nodes orbit a glowing nucleus. Clicking a node expands a detail card showing its
description, status, energy level, and connected nodes. Connections pulse to
highlight relationships. The orbit pauses on interaction and resumes automatically.

All content (nodes, dates, status, energy, connections) is managed in the Odoo
backend under the **Orbital Timeline** menu.
